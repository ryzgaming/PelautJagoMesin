Marine Operations Division
The YANMAR Marine Operations Division specializes in developing
and producing small and medium-sized diesel engines mainly at the
Tsukaguchi Plant. From processing of components for
marine propulsion engines, marine auxiliary engines,
land and industrial engines to assembly, rigging, and test runs,
the Tsukaguchi Plant uses a consistent quality
control system to produce a wide range of diesel engines.
